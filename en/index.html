About the Program

Mission

Get Seen. Connect with Capital.
TiBOOST Innovation Growth Program — Your Launchpad to Taiwan's Capital Market!

The TiBOOST competition welcomes applications from high-potential startups across Asia and around the world, with approximately 10 to 12 companies selected for the final round. Through forum discussions, international competition, and cross-sector collaboration, the program connects innovative technologies, industry needs, and capital-market opportunities. It also helps promising startups engage with Taiwan's investment ecosystem and explore future listing opportunities on the Taiwan Innovation Board.

By strengthening Taiwan's position as a leading Asian hub for innovation, fundraising, and public listings, TiBOOST supports Taiwan's long-term vision of becoming the "Nasdaq of Asia" and a gateway connecting Asia's innovation ecosystem with global capital markets.

﹝ ELIGIBILITY · ALL CRITERIA MUST BE MET ﹞
  • 1.The applicant must be a legally incorporated startup company in Taiwan or another country or region. A company incorporated or registered in Mainland China is not eligible to apply. For a company incorporated in a third country, third area, or other overseas jurisdiction, its ownership structure and controlling relationships must comply with Article 3 of Taiwan's Regulations Governing Permission for Investment by People of the Mainland Area. Individuals, legal entities, organizations, or other institutions from Mainland China may not directly or indirectly hold more than 30% of the applicant company's shares or total contributed capital, nor may they otherwise exercise control over the applicant company. Applicants must provide corporate registration documents, ownership and shareholding information, and any other relevant supporting documents required by the organizer.
  • 2.Each company may submit only one application. There is no limit on the number of team members included in an application. Every participating member must be at least 18 years old by the application deadline.
  • 3.
    The applicant's technology or product must fall within at least one of the priority areas supported under Taiwan's Ten Major AI Infrastructure Initiatives:
    • AI-enabled frontier technologies, such as silicon photonics, quantum technologies, and intelligent robotics
    • AI applications for industry, such as smart manufacturing, fintech, and smart retail
    • AI-powered smart applications, such as smart healthcare, smart cities, and intelligent transportation
    • Core AI technologies, such as generative AI, AIoT, and edge AI
    • AI infrastructure and platforms, such as computing platforms, data governance, and AI cybersecurity
  • 4.The applicant must have completed at least one external funding round or demonstrated initial market validation, such as revenue or users.
  • 5.The applicant must not have previously been listed on the Taiwan Innovation Board.

﹝ APPLICATIONS ﹞

Startups incorporated in Taiwan or other eligible countries or regions may apply, provided that they meet all requirements set out in the Competition Guidelines.

All application materials must be submitted by 12:00 noon on September 29, 2026, Taiwan Time (UTC+8). Late submissions will not be accepted.

In principle, each company may submit only one application. Please refer to the Competition Guidelines for detailed rules.

To request revisions during the application period, please contact the organizer. Applications generally cannot be changed after the application deadline.

﹝ COMPETITION ﹞

Following eligibility screening and application review, approximately 10 to 12 companies will be selected as finalists. Finalists will deliver an in-person pitch presentation and participate in a Q&A session with the judging panel at the TiBOOST Innovation Growth Program Final and Awards Ceremony.

Applications will be evaluated based on the following criteria: market opportunity, solution, business model and market validation, capital-market readiness, and team capabilities. The judging panel may also consider available evidence of customer adoption, product or business-model validation, revenue performance, fundraising progress, and other operating achievements.

In addition to cash prizes, winning companies may receive opportunities for: access to startup hub facilities, media and promotional exposure, business and investor matchmaking, connections with Taiwan's capital market, venture capital firms, and industry partners, and increased international exposure and fundraising opportunities.
